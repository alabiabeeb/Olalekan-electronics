Our journey

From a small Ogunpa shop to a trusted name

Founded in 2020, WIZZYMONEY Olamileken Electronics began as a small electronics store in Ibadan with a simple goal: provide quality electronics at fair prices.

Since then we’ve grown from basic home appliances to smart TVs, sound systems, energy-efficient refrigerators, generators and solar — building lasting relationships with customers along the way.

We keep the personal touch that defined our beginnings: every product is checked before it leaves the store, and you can always reach a real person.
